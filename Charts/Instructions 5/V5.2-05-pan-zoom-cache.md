# V5.2 Prompt 05 - Pan/Zoom Cache Stability

Goal
- Make pan/zoom feel locked and jitter-free under load.

Constraints
- Preserve current visual output.
- Maintain axis stability during drag.

Deliverables
- Stable cache reuse rules.
- No visible jitter or axis wobble while dragging.

Steps
1) Review pan cache invalidation rules and overscan usage.
2) Ensure axis updates during drag do not cause discontinuities.
3) Validate cache redraw when overscan exceeded.

Acceptance
- Pan/zoom is smooth and visually stable.
- No ghosting or residual lines.

Tests
