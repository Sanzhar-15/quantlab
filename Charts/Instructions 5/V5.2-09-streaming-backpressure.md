# V5.2 Prompt 09 - Streaming Backpressure

Goal
- Prevent render storms under bursty updates.

Constraints
- Must preserve data correctness.
- Backpressure behavior must be configurable.

Deliverables
- Backpressure policy for appendBatch/updateLast.
- Stable per-frame invalidation during heavy streaming.

Steps
1) Apply backpressure/coalescing on DataProvider updates.
2) Ensure batching produces at most one invalidation per frame.
3) Validate no data loss unless explicitly configured.

Acceptance
- Scenario E shows stable latency and bounded render counts.
- No data correctness regressions.

Tests
- Perf scenario E + targeted streaming unit tests.
