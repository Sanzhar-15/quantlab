V4 PR2 - Input pipeline and scheduler coalescing.

Goal:
- Reduce drag/zoom jitter and input latency without breaking API.

Tasks:
- Use event.timeStamp (fall back to performance.now) for pan velocity and inertia timing.
- Coalesce pointer events into a single per-frame intent while preserving total delta.
- Ensure pointerrawupdate only feeds the FrameScheduler (no direct rendering).
- Avoid overlay invalidations when pointer is outside the plot and crosshair is inactive.
- Keep pointermove overlay-only invariant intact.

Acceptance:
- Drag input latency improves in perf scenario A/B.
- No regressions in crosshair behavior or inertia feel.
