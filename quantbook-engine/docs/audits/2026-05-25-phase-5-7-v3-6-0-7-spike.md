# Phase 5.7 V3.6.0.7 — D6 profiling spike (workbookSnapshot cost)

**Date**: 2026-05-25
**Branch**: `feat/quantbook-engine`
**Engine HEAD entering**: `1e1e5464da9` (V3.6.0.X audit-of-D5 closures)
**Spike type**: time-boxed 1-session measurement (per V3.6.0.1 decision lock § D6)
**Verdict**: **D6 SHIPS in V3.6.0.8.**

## Purpose

The V3.6.0.1 decision lock (`.plans/_active.md` § D6) defers the choice to ship `workbookSnapshotDelta(last_seen_version)` until mid-V3.6 profiling. Decision rule:

- median cost at 100k cells > 50 ms/call → ship D6 (estimated 3 sessions).
- median cost ≤ 50 ms/call → defer D6 to V3.7+.

The measurement must capture **post-D2 + post-D4** cost (the V3.6.0.3 RegisterFormat cache + V3.6.0.5 format-aware rendering both add per-snapshot iteration work on top of the V3.5 baseline). The 50 ms threshold reflects the IDE redraw-loop budget: at 60 fps the frame budget is 16.7 ms; even at 30 fps a redraw budget is 33 ms; > 50 ms is a perceptible hitch on the user-driven write path.

## Methodology

A criterion bench at `crates/ql-bindings-node/benches/workbook_snapshot.rs` replicates the body of `crates/ql-bindings-node/src/lib.rs:1802 workbook_snapshot` in pure Rust. napi methods can't be invoked from a Cargo bench harness without bootstrapping Node; the replicated body is the practical equivalent.

Drift defence: setup-time assertion in the bench compares the replicated body's cell count to the workload generator's expected cell count for all 12 configurations. The assertion fired clean on every run (zero panics in the bench output).

## Decision

**Decision combo (`cells=100 000, format=50 %, sheets=1`) median = 251.38 ms ≈ 5× the 50 ms threshold.** D6 SHIPS in V3.6.0.8.

Secondary signals supporting the decision:

- **D4 cost is real but not the dominant term.** At 100 k cells the format=50 % case (251 ms) is ~65 % more expensive than the format=0 % case (152 ms). The extra ~99 ms covers ~50 k cell format::render calls plus ~50 k more ops in the replay log (each `Op::SetCellFormat` adds one log entry). That's roughly 2 μs per render + replay-overhead — consistent with the post-D4 per-snapshot `parsed_format_cache` working as designed (one parse per distinct FormatId; 3 distinct formats here so cache hits dominate).

- **Cost is super-linear in cell count.** 10 k → 100 k cells (10×) takes 25.1 ms → 251.4 ms (10.0×) in the format=50 % series; 14.97 ms → 152.4 ms (10.2×) in the format=0 % series. Effective scaling is linear-to-slightly-superlinear; rebuild_workbook + sheet_repair walks dominate.

- **The threshold breakpoint is between 10 k and 50 k cells.** At 10 k cells (50% format, 1 sheet), 25 ms is well under 50 ms. The threshold crosses at ~20 k cells extrapolated — anything beyond a small spreadsheet runs the risk of perceptible redraw lag without D6.

- **Multi-sheet doesn't make things worse per-cell**; it makes them worse by total cells. 10 k cells × 5 sheets (50 k cells total, 81 ms) is slightly cheaper per cell than 10 k cells × 1 sheet × 5 = (50 k cells total, ~125 ms extrapolated) because sheet_repair walks are bounded by sheet count, not cell count. D6 will benefit large-single-sheet workbooks proportionally more.

- **The cost is unavoidably per-call.** `workbookSnapshot` is called on every IDE redraw cycle that needs the current full state (initial render + post-undo + post-remote-merge + post-edit refresh). D6's incremental delta API (changes since last `version_vector`) skips both `rebuild_workbook` and the per-cell iteration for unchanged cells.

## Risk register update

- **R-V3.6-5 incremental snapshot delta freshness** (already documented in V3.6.0.1 § D6): when D6 ships, the API must fall back to full snapshot when version-vector predecessor is unreachable (Loro may GC old history).

- **R-V3.6-NEW (this spike) rebuild_workbook is the dominant cost.** D6 ship plan must include a strategy for the rebuild_workbook portion — either (a) caching the prior rebuilt Workbook and diffing forward from there, (b) reading directly from the V3.4.0.2 incremental snapshot cache + skipping rebuild_workbook (works only if no rename-repair changed since last call), or (c) something else. Locked at D6 ship.

## Out-of-scope for this spike

- D6 implementation shape — locked at D6 ship.
- rebuild_workbook optimization as a standalone improvement — V3.7+ if D6 still leaves headroom.
- format::render isolated profiling — not necessary because D6 covers it via per-call avoidance.
